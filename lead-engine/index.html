Eclipso Lead Engine

Eclipso Lead Engine helps Eclipso Digital organize published business registration records, prepare approved service introductions, and manage outreach history through its own authorized Gmail account.

This page describes the tool. It is not a public sign-in or mailbox-connection portal. We do not ask visitors or email recipients for access to their Google accounts.

What the application does

  • Collects approved public business registration and contact records.
  • Records qualification checks and excludes previously contacted, opted-out, held, or known-website contacts.
  • Sends approved introductory emails only after the operator enables sending and within the configured daily ceiling.
  • Checks tracked correspondence for replies, opt-outs, and delivery failures so the operator can manage responses manually.

Why it uses Google permissions

Basic Google identity permissions confirm the approved Eclipso account. Gmail send permission enables approved introductions. Gmail read-only permission supports reply, opt-out, and delivery-report checks. The application does not automatically answer replies, send follow-ups, or measure email opens.

Data handling and controls

The cloud worker runs on Supabase without requiring an AI model. Application records include sources, recipients, send identifiers, timestamps, and workflow statuses; full Gmail message bodies are not stored in the application database. Credentials are kept out of the public website. The operator can pause sending or revoke Google access.

Read the Privacy Policy for access, use, storage, sharing, retention, and deletion information, and the Terms of Use for operating limits.

Questions or an outreach opt-out?

Contact eclipsodigital@gmail.com. To stop outreach, reply “no thanks” to an introduction. Receiving an email does not connect your mailbox to this tool.
