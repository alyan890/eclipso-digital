Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This policy describes Eclipso Digital's public website and its private Eclipso Lead Engine. For privacy questions or requests, contact eclipsodigital@gmail.com.

The Lead Engine connects only to Eclipso Digital's own authorized Gmail account. Website visitors and businesses receiving an introduction do not need to sign in with Google or grant access to their mailboxes.

1. Website enquiries and browser information

If you use the website contact form, it submits your name, email address, and message to Web3Forms for enquiry handling. If you email us, your message is handled through our email provider. Do not include passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive information in an enquiry.

The main website stores theme and promotional-popup preferences in your browser's local storage. You can remove these through your browser settings. The main website loads fonts from Google Fonts. Our hosting, font, and form providers may process technical information such as IP addresses and request details to operate their services. These policy pages do not load third-party fonts or tracking scripts.

2. Public business lead information

The Lead Engine collects business names, published business contact emails, registration dates, industry categories, and registry location fields from approved public business registries. It stores source links and qualification records. A registry email is not proof of deliverability, and a recent registration is not proof of a new launch or absence of a website.

3. Google account and Gmail permissions

With the account owner's authorization, the application uses basic Google identity permissions to verify the connected account, Gmail send permission to send approved introductions, and Gmail read-only permission to identify replies, opt-outs, and delivery reports. The read-only permission technically permits mailbox reading; the application's workflow uses message metadata to locate relevant correspondence and processes replies and delivery reports associated with tracked outreach.

Gmail message content is processed by the cloud worker to classify reply and delivery status. The application database stores recipient addresses, message and thread identifiers, timestamps, sending outcomes, reply classifications, contact holds, and opt-out records. It does not store full Gmail message bodies. The worker does not automatically answer replies or send follow-ups. The account owner can read and answer messages manually in Gmail.

4. Purpose and limits on Google data use

Google data is used to operate the authorized outreach workflow, verify account identity, track correspondence, and prevent further outreach to opted-out contacts. We do not sell Google user data or use it for advertising profiles. The automated Lead Engine does not send Gmail data to generative-AI services or use it to train AI models.

Eclipso Lead Engine follows the Google API Services User Data Policy and its Limited Use requirements when handling information obtained through Google APIs.

5. Processing providers and access

Google provides account authorization and Gmail delivery. Supabase hosts the private cloud worker and application database and processes the data needed for those functions. The website is hosted on Vercel, and its contact form uses Web3Forms. Providers may process information in locations outside your country under their own service terms and privacy policies.

Access to operational records is restricted to the authorized operator and necessary service infrastructure. We may also disclose information when legally required or necessary to address fraud, abuse, or security incidents. These disclosures are subject to applicable restrictions on Google data.

6. Credentials and security

Long-lived credentials saved locally are protected with Windows user encryption outside the website and source repository. Cloud credentials are kept in server-side secret storage, not in browser code. Private application tables use access controls and row-level security. Network requests use HTTPS except for temporary, loopback-only authorization forms on the operator's own computer. No system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Retention, opt-outs, and deletion requests

Operational lead and correspondence records are retained to manage outreach and prevent duplicate contact. The current system does not have an automatic deletion timetable. Minimal suppression records may be retained to honor opt-outs. To stop outreach, reply “no thanks” to an introduction or email the address above. To request access, correction, or deletion of information associated with your business or email, contact us; requests are handled manually, taking account of identity checks, suppression needs, and applicable obligations.

8. Disconnecting Google and policy updates

The account owner can revoke Eclipso Lead Engine's Google access through Google Account connections. Revocation prevents further authorized Google API access but does not itself erase records already stored by the application. The operator can separately remove the cloud credentials and retained data. We will update this page if our data practices change.
